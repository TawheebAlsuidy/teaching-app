2.11 Armenia - 7, December 1988
Look at the photographs and read the newsflashes and information about the disaster.
من الكتابu2-2_11-newsflashes · reading · PB ص 16
13.00 Flash. Earthquake in Armenia. Hit town of Spivak at 11.41 local time. 6.9 on the Richter scale. First reports say that damage is serious.
14.00 Armenia: Considerable loss of life reported. 'The flats were very badly built,' said an eye-witness. 'They fell down like packs of cards.' The rescue workers have no equipment and are working with their bare hands.
15.00 Armenia: Spivak completely flattened. Most of population - 20,000 - killed. In Leninakan, Armenia's largest city, thousands of people are homeless. One in five buildings destroyed. 'We urgently need tents and blankets, a spokesman said.
16.00 Earthquake Epidemic Risk: Rescue workers say there is a danger of cholera and typhoid following the Armenian earthquake. 'There is no clean drinking water,' said a nurse. 'Disease can spread very quickly. We need clean water now.
17.00 Earthquake: The Armenian earthquake is the strongest since 1964 when an earthquake measuring 8.6 on the Richter scale hit Alaska.
23.00 President to return: President Gorbachov is interrupting his trip to the USA to take charge of rescue efforts. Officials number of dead: 55,000. Some say this could reach 100,000.
Photo captions: The earthquake was too strong for this badly-built block of flats. | Rescue workers look for survivors. | Homeless people keep warm around a fire.
إضافة تعليمية: ملاحظات نقل حرفي (PB p. 16):

من الكتابu2-2_11-richter · table · PB ص 16
The Richter scale: The strength of an earthquake is measured on the Richter scale. It was invented by Charles Richter, an American scientist.
More than 8.0 → Almost total damage
More than 7.4 → Great damage
7.0 - 7.3 → Serious damage, metal bridges bent
6.2 - 6.9 → Considerable damage to buildings
5.5 - 6.1 → Slight damage to buildings

q-2_11-1 · mcq · دليل: u2-2_11-richter
According to the Richter scale table on PB page 16, what level of damage corresponds to an earthquake measuring 6.9 on the Richter scale (like the 1988 Armenia earthquake)?
  • Considerable damage to buildings (6.2 - 6.9) ✓
  • Slight damage to buildings (5.5 - 6.1) ✗ — نطاق 5.5–6.1 أقل من 6.9 في الجدول.
  • Serious damage, metal bridges bent (7.0 - 7.3) ✗ — يبدأ نطاق Serious damage من 7.0، بينما سجل زلزال أرمينيا 6.9 في برقية 13.00 (مع أن الأضرار الواقعية كانت جسيمة لرداءة البناء كما قالت برقية 14.00).
  • Almost total damage (More than 8.0) ✗ — الأكثر من 8.0 يخص زلزال ألاسكا 1964 (8.6) في برقية 17.00.
q-2_11-2 · mcq · دليل: u2-2_11-newsflashes
Which two newsflashes in PB page 16 mention lack of clean water / cholera-typhoid and rescue workers working without equipment?

  • 13.00 and 17.00 ✗ — 13.00 تبلغ عن وقت الهزة الأولى و17.00 تقارنها بزلزال ألاسكا 1964.
  • 15.00 and 23.00 ✗ — 15.00 تتحدث عن الخيام والبطانيات وتدمير سبيفاك، و23.00 عن عودة الرئيس غورباتشوف.
