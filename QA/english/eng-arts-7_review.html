ARTS 7   Telford Hall Episode 3 — Part 1 (PB p. 58)
[Complete story pages available offline in assets/ref/pb_p058.webp & pb_p059.webp; structured column-line anchors below matching WB Arts 7 A–F:]
Col. 1 (Exam morning & hall, PB p. 58): On a brightly sunny examination morning ('Lovely day,' — 'It always is when there's an examination'), Ahmed and Bob walked through college gardens to the main hall. At 9.45 doors opened, students poured into rows of desks with name cards and face-down exam papers. An unfamiliar lecturer supervised the three-hour exams. By Friday afternoon Ahmed's arm ached but he felt happy exams were over.
Col. 2 (Sunday weekend with Bob's family, PB p. 58): Waiting for results the following week ('until then'), Ahmed drove Bob home in his black London taxi after Bob's parents phoned ('They would like you to come home'). On Sunday morning, Ahmed, Bob and Bob's mother walked along the beach while Bob's father stayed home cooking Sunday roast beef ('He always cooks Sunday lunch... doesn't like being disturbed'). Seeing high sand dunes beside the beach reminded Ahmed of home ('They remind me of home'). Back home, lunch tasted delicious ('It tasted delicious as well'). Afterwards everyone washed up together ('Many hands make light work,' said Bob's mother). Bob's sister and family called in to say 'hello'; when they had gone ('When they had gone'), the men played a word game and all watched an old film. Just before falling asleep, Ahmed thought how much he enjoyed spending 'a quite Sunday' in Bob's home — 'It made him miss his own family.'
Results Board transition: They returned to Norton the day the examination results were posted on the main building notice board.
من الكتابarts-7-episode3-p59 · reading · PB ص 59
ARTS 7   Telford Hall Episode 3 — Part 2 (PB p. 59)
Exam Results: Bob worried because he could not see their names among the B and C grades; another student pointed to the top under grade A: Robert Wilson and Ahmed Hassan Al-Hadrami! Ahmed said he was going home 'The day after tomorrow.'
Ahmed's Thank-you Letter (Telford Hall, Norton. Friday.): 'Dear Mr and Mrs Wilson, Thank you very much for having me to stay for the past few days. I had a wonderful time and you really made me feel like one of the family. I hope one day that I will be able to repay your kindness and hospitality. Thank you again. Best wishes, Ahmed'
Departure from Norton: At rainy Norton Central station, Ahmed asked Bob to look after the taxi ('Of course, 'said Bob. 'Don't worry about a thing.'). Boarding the London train, Ahmed opened the window and shouted 'See you in October ...' as Bob waved. Watching countryside that had seemed strange nine months earlier, Ahmed reached Euston station and four hours later looked out of his plane over London before heading home ten hours away ('through the clouds Ten hours later...').
Discussion: 1 Does your family spend Fridays in a traditional way? 2 Compare your Fridays with English Saturdays.
