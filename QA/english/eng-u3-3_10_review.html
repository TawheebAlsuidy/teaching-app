3.10 Language review 6
1 Nouns, adjectives and verbs + prepositions
Some adjectives, nouns and verbs are commonly followed by prepositions. These are the ones you have seen in Unit 3:
Nouns: training in | experience of | chance of
Adjectives: interested in | grateful for
Verbs: look forward to | applying for
Examples: I would like to apply for the job. | I have experience of working with children.
Note: They are always followed by either a noun or a gerund (the -ing form).
من الكتابu3-3_10-connectors · rule · PB ص 23
2 Addition
Addition, here, means giving information. To do this, we use connecting words, • inside a sentence, • at the end of a sentence, • at the beginning of a sentence.
Examples: He wore a hat and a coat as well as/together with woollen gloves. | He wore a hat and a coat. He put on woollen gloves as well/also. | She is polite and friendly. Furthermore, [In addition,] she is very conscientious. | Another reason for giving her the job is her patience.
Note: also can be used inside a sentence. He also put on woollen gloves.
3 Consequence
Consequence is about one idea being the result of another. The expressions used to connect the ideas are as follows:
because | so | therefore | thus (followed by the word ending -ing) | consequently | as a consequence | as a result | accordingly | that is why
Find examples of this language in the texts.

الأسئلة والاختبارات الذاتية

q-3_10-1 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_10-prep
Choose the grammatically correct completion from PB page 23: 'I look forward ______ from you in the near future.'
  • to hearing ✓
  • to hear ✗ — كلمة to في look forward to حرف جر يجب أن يتبعه اسم أو gerund (-ing)، وليس مصدراً مجرداً.
  • for hearing ✗ — حرف الجر الثابت مع look forward هو to وليس for.
  • hearing ✗ — لا يُحذف حرف الجر to من التركيب look forward to.
q-3_10-2 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_10-connectors
According to PB page 23, which consequence connector requires the -ing form right after it?
  • thus ✓
  • because ✗ — يتبع because جملة كاملة (فاعل + فعل)، لا فعل بصيغة -ing مباشرة.
  • furthermore ✗ — furthermore من روابط الإضافة لا النتيجة، ويتبعها جملة كاملة.
  • therefore ✗ — يتبع therefore جملة كاملة (فاعل + فعل)، بينما نص الكتاب على (thus followed by the word ending -ing).
