3.6 Job and qualities
Names of jobs
Some names of jobs describe what the person does very clearly. For example, taxi-driver. The two nouns combine to make one word and are called compounds.
Sometimes they are joined: shopkeeper
Sometimes they have a hyphen: life-guard
Sometimes they stay as two words: English teacher
Say what each of these people does in their job:
Example: taxi-driver: A taxi-driver dives taxis.
Note: In American English programme is always spelt 'program'. In British English this spelling is used only in connection with computers. It is both a noun and a verb.
Job box: tourist guide | firefighter | bookseller | taxi-driver | computer programmer | dressmaker | film director | newsreader | science teacher | bank manager | project manager
In 'taxi-driver' there is no 's' at the end of taxi. When nouns are used as adjectives, They are always singular.
Find other examples in the list. Can you think of any others?
إضافة تعليمية: ملاحظات نقل حرفي (PB p. 21): كُتب العنوان Job and qualities بصيغة المفرد (بينما جاء في فهرس الكتاب Jobs and qualities)، كما طُبعت كلمة dives بدلاً من drives في A taxi-driver dives taxis، وبدأ الضمير They بحرف كبير بعد الفاصلة في adjectives, They are always singular؛ وحُفظت جميعها حرفياً.

q-3_6-1 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_6-compounds
According to PB page 21 ('When nouns are used as adjectives...'), what is the correct compound job title for 'somebody who tames lions'?
  • a lion-tamer ✓
  • a lions-tamer ✗ — تقرّر قاعدة PB ص 21 أن الاسم الأول المستخدم كصفة يبقى مفرداً دائماً دون -s.
  • a tame-lion ✗ — يتقدم المفعول (lion) ويلحق الفاعل المضاف إليه -er (tamer) على نمط taxi-driver.
  • a lions-tame ✗ — تجمع هذه الصيغة الخطأ في الترتيب والجمع معاً.
q-3_6-2 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_6-qualities
What is the noun form corresponding to the adjective 'computer literate' on PB page 21?
  • computer literacy ✓
  • computer literateness ✗ — الاسم المقابل المعتمد في جدول PB ص 21 هو computer literacy وليس بإضافة -ness.
  • computer literature ✗ — literature تعني الأدب، لا الإلمام بالحاسوب.
  • computer literates ✗ — هذه ليست صيغة المصدر/الصفة المطلوبة في الجدول.
