ARTS 1   A difficult choice?
[Complete illustrated narrative text available offline in assets/ref/pb_p051.webp; structured paragraph anchors & exact quotes below for WB Arts 1 A–E:]
Introduction box (verbatim cue lines): Olivia Lee was travelling in a taxi when the taxi-driver told her he had 11-year-old twin daughters, one very ill with kidney disease. 'Why don't you give her one of your kidneys?' asked Olivia, 'or her sister could give her one'. 'What would you know about it? asked the taxi-driver. Olivia told him she was a kidney donor for her brother; he stopped the taxi to hear her story.
Para 1 (Meeting Michael & Two choices): After not seeing her brother Michael for a long time, athletic and lively Michael was now thin, tired and ill with a life-threatening kidney infection ('He had a two choices.': wait for a matching kidney from someone who died for a transplant, or spend the rest of his life connected to a kidney machine).
Para 2 (Olivia's decision): Olivia immediately decided to donate one kidney, knowing some people are born with one and live normal lives.
Para 3 (Doctor's warning): At hospital the doctor explained surgery always carries small risk for a healthy person, but would be more serious for Olivia: cutting deeply into her body, leaving a 30-centimetre scar, staying in hospital a week to ten days, then resting three to four weeks.
Para 4–6 (Outcome & Advice): Nevertheless, the operation succeeded; Michael leads a healthy life and Olivia feels no different despite her scar. Her advice to the taxi-driver: 'If you are close to somebody, don't hold back. It's worth the pain and the small risk.' (True story with names changed.)
Discussion: From what you now know, would you donate one of your kidney to a brother or sister?

In WB Arts 1 D #7, what is the correct direct-speech version of the doctor's words 'He would have to cut deeply into Olivia's body'?
  • 'I will have to cut deeply into your body.' ✓
  • 'He will have to cut deeply into your body.' ✗ — الفاعل He هو الطبيب نفسه المتحدث، لذا يصبح في المباشر I.
  • 'You will have to cut deeply into my body.' ✗ — عكست الضمائر: الجرّاح هو من يجري الشق الجراحي لا المريضة.
  • 'I would cut deeply into Olivia's body.' ✗ — لم يُرجَع الفعل would have إلى will have ولم يتحول الاسم للمخاطَب your.
