ARTS 5   Telford Hall Episode 2 — Part 1 (PB p. 55)
[Complete story pages available offline in assets/ref/pb_p055.webp & pb_p056.webp; full scene anchors below for WB Arts 5 A–G:]
Christmas Break & Liverpool Visit: As winter approached and days got shorter, Ahmed realized he would soon be by himself for three weeks. He suddenly missed home: warm evenings walking on the beach, camping in the desert, his friends, and his mother's evening cooking. While looking at the ceiling, Bob knocked and passed on his parents' invitation to spend Christmas with them near Liverpool. Later in the Telford Hall lounge, Ahmed wrote 'Dear Khaled' telling his brother about Liverpool, the surrounding countryside, and Christmas Day when Bob's older sister Jenny came with her husband Graham and two children (it felt strange hearing children call Bob 'Uncle').
Ramadan & Eid al-Fitr Party: One morning Ahmed invited Bob to eat in his room, explaining Ramadan (fasting dawn to dusk as a duty) and Eid al-Fitr ('asked Bob.Another explanation followed.'). He had also invited Mick, Jerry and Derek and a couple of others. Ahmed pushed all the furniture to one end of his room around a brass tray from his parents, piled high with meat and rice, plus two dishes of sweet pastry with honey and nuts — made after giving his recipes and spices to the Telford Hall cooks and helping them all afternoon. Earlier in the day he phoned his family to wish them a good Eid, played cassettes from home, passed around sweet pastry, and felt good celebrating Eid al-Fitr with his new friends.

ARTS 5   Telford Hall Episode 2 — Part 2: Stratford-upon-Avon (PB pp. 55–56)
Weekend Before Exams: Before sitting six three-hour end-of-year exam papers (morning and afternoon over three days), Ahmed took a weekend off with Bob to visit Stratford-upon-Avon (about three hours' drive from Norton).
Shakespeare's Birthplace & River Avon: On Saturday morning they visited the actual 16th-century house where William Shakespeare was born on April 23rd, 1564, seeing heavy wooden furniture, 16th-century kitchen cooking equipment, the birth room and Shakespeare's school desk. They stayed at the local Youth Hostel and spent Sunday morning rowing on the River Avon (which gives the town its name).
Royal Shakespeare Theatre & Souvenirs: Ahmed pointed to the huge brick Royal Shakespeare Theatre ('They do three of four different ones every year', out of Shakespeare's 37 plays). In tourist shops selling Shakespeare teapots, dolls and toothbrushes, Ahmed bought a photo book of the town, copies of Shakespeare's plays, and a special mirror painted with Shakespeare's beard, eyebrows and hair ('I'll give it Khaled,' said Ahmed. 'I think he will like it.').
Discussion: Explain Eid Al-Fitr in English.
