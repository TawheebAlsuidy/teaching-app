6.10 Discovering Yemen
Talk about the picture.
Describe what you can see - first the two four-wheel-drives in the foreground and then the things further away in the distance.
Now say what you think about the picture. Where must it have been taken? Who do you think the people in the four-wheel-drives are? Where might they be going? Give reasons.
Now do activities A to B in the Workbook.

q-6_10-1 · mcq · دليل: u6-6_10-picture-talk
How does the description order requested on PB page 48 (6.10) differ from PB page 42 (6.2)?
  • Lesson 6.10 starts with the foreground (the two four-wheel-drives) and moves to the distance, whereas 6.2 starts furthest away. ✓
  • Lesson 6.10 describes only the background sky and ignores the vehicles. ✗ — يبدأ التوجيه صراحةً بسيارتَي الدفع الرباعي في المقدمة ثم ما يبعد في الأفق.
  • Both 6.2 and 6.10 strictly forbid mentioning the foreground. ✗ — كلا الدرسين يستعملان التعبيرين In the foreground وIn the distance.
  • Lesson 6.10 uses Past passive to describe surgical operations. ✗ — هذا يصف درس 6.5 عن عملية أنور لا مشهد اكتشاف اليمن.
q-6_10-2 · mcq · دليل: u6-6_10-picture-talk
In PB page 48, which detail in the illustration best supports the deduction 'The people in the four-wheel-drives might be tourists travelling far'?
  • Both 4WDs are packed with luggage on their roof racks on a steep mountain track passing traditional hillside houses. ✓
  • They are parked outside a harbour fishing boat called D173. ✗ — قارب الصيد D173 يخص مشهد الميناء في الوحدة 5 (5.1).
  • A police highway patrol officer is interviewing a motorbike rider beside a tunnel. ✗ — هذا يخص مشهد الحادث في درس 6.2.
  • Doctors are examining a patient on an ambulance stretcher. ✗ — هذا يخص لوحات إنقاذ أنور في درس 6.4.
