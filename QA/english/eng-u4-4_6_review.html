4.6 Language review 7
1 Use of sequence words and phrases (first, then, next, after that, finally)
You use these words to introduce each stage of a process.
Examples: First you fry the onions and garlic. | Then you add the spices. | Next you add the tomatoes. | After that you cover the mixture. | Finally you simmer for fifteen minutes.
Note: some writers put a comma after each of these sequence words/phrases. Example: First, put some water in a pan.
2 Use of the Passive
You use the Passive when you want to make the object more important than the subject.
Example: subject: People | object: grow coffee in Yemen. → Coffee is grown in Yemen.
Example: subject: We can find | object: fruit trees in some wadis. → Fruit trees can be found in some wadis
Note: We often omit the original subject in the passive sentence, particularly when it is not important, as in the above examples 'people' and 'we'.
إضافة تعليمية: ملاحظة نقل حرفي (PB p. 28 وWB p. 46): سقطت النقطة في آخر مثال Fruit trees can be found in some wadis بـ PB p. 28؛ كما وردت في WB 4.6 D مسافة قبل القوس في ( wait) وغياب المسافة قبل (heat)، وحُفظت جميعها حرفياً.
من الكتابu4-4_6-while · rule · PB ص 28
3 Use of while + Present continuous with Present simple
You use while + Present continuous with the Present simple when you want to show that two actions happen at the same time.
Example: First action: While the mixture is boiling gently, | Second action: you fry the pieces of fish.

q-4_6-1 · mcq · دليل: u4-4_6-seq-passive
In WB 4.6 B, what is the correct Present passive conversion of 'Next you add the tomatoes.'?
  • Next the tomatoes are added. ✓
  • Next the tomatoes is added. ✗ — كلمة tomatoes جمع فتلزم الفعل المساعد are وليس is.
  • Next the tomatoes are adding. ✗ — المبني للمجهول يحتاج التصريف الثالث added وليس صيغة -ing.
  • Next you are added the tomatoes. ✗ — في المجهول نحذف الفاعل you ونقدّم المفعول به (the tomatoes).
q-4_6-2 · mcq · دليل: u4-4_6-while
Choose the correct verb forms for WB 4.6 D #3: 'You ______ (make) the sauce while the food ______ (cook).'

  • are making ... cooks ✗ — قاعدة PB ص 28 تربط while نفسها بالمضارع المستمر (while the food is cooking)، والفعل الآخر بالبسيط (You make).
  • make ... cook ✗ — الجملة التي تلي while تستلزم المضارع المستمر (is cooking).
