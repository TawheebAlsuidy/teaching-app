5.2 Possibilities
Three children are looking at something that they have found. They are trying to work out what it is.
Look at what they are saying.
Girl 1: It might be a coin.
Boy 1: Or it may be a piece of jewellery.
Boy 2: You're right. It could be an earring.
Girl 1: No, look. There is a number here. It must be a coin
Talk about what these objects might be.
Close-up Objects 1–6 (illustrated photos strip): 1 toothbrush bristles | 2 shower head holes | 3 clinical thermometer (37–38 scale) | 4 screw thread / light-bulb base | 5 pair of glasses | 6 bottle screw top
إضافة تعليمية: ملاحظات نقل حرفي (PB p. 34 وWB p. 57):
1) في فقاعة PB p. 34 سقطت النقطة آخر جملة It must be a coin.
2) في WB 5.2 B #4 طُبعت عبارة we have (have to) ______ to use oil lamps. بتكرار have قبل القوس وتكرار to بعد الفراغ؛ حُفظت الصيغة المطبوعة حرفياً مع بيان الجواب الصحيح (would have to use oil lamps).
من الكتابu5-5_2-what-if · dialogue · PB ص 34

What would happen if ...
1 ... there was no more rain in Yemen?
2 ... all planes stopped flying?
3 ... all plants stopped growing?
4 ... there was no more electricity?
Boy: I've no idea. / I haven't a clue.
Girl: If there was no more rain in Yemen, the whole country would turn to desert.

q-5_2-1 · mcq · دليل: u5-5_2-what-if
In PB page 34, what idiomatic expression has the same meaning as 'I've no idea.'?
  • I haven't a clue. ✓
  • It must be a coin. ✗ — هذه تفيد اليقين بالإجابة لا عدم المعرفة.
  • Anyone can see that. ✗ — هذه تفيد الوضوح التام لا الجهل بالأمر.
  • And I was right. ✗ — هذه تفيد تحقق التوقع لا غياب الفكرة.
q-5_2-2 · mcq · دليل: u5-5_2-what-if
Complete WB 5.2 B #2 cleanly: 'If all the planes ______ (stop) flying, hotels ______ (have) fewer customers.'
  • stopped ... would have ✓
  • stop ... will have ✗ — يتحدث التمرين عن افتراض تخيلي مستبعد بـ What would happen if... لذا يستعمل الشرط الثاني.
  • stopped ... had ✗ — جواب الشرط الثاني يحتاج would + المصدر (would have).
  • would stop ... have ✗ — لا تأتي would في جملة فعل الشرط المباشرة بعد If.
