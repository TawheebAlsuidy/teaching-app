الأسئلة والاختبارات الذاتية

q-6_5-1 · mcq · دليل: u6-6_5-process-notes
In WB 6.5 B, which sentence requires the Past simple PASSIVE form because its grammatical subject received the action?
  • Anwar (give) first-aid / village clinic → Anwar was given first-aid at the village clinic. ✓
  • She (rush) Anwar / hospital → She was rushed Anwar to hospital. ✗ — الفاعل She (سلوى) هو من قام بالإسراع فالصحيح المعلوم: She rushed Anwar.
  • parents (come) to take / home → His parents were come to take him home. ✗ — الفعل come لازم والآباء هم الفاعلون فالصحيح: His parents came.
  • very good heart surgeon (operate) → A surgeon was operated on Anwar. ✗ — الجراح هو الفاعل الذي أجرى العملية فالصحيح المعلوم: operated on Anwar.
q-6_5-2 · mcq · دليل: u6-6_5-process-notes
In WB 6.5 A, what comes immediately after note 2 ('She rushed Anwar to hospital in Hajjah by car')?
  • 3: The doctor examined him and decided he needed specialist treatment. ✓
  • Anwar was taken to Sana'a by ambulance before anyone examined him. ✗ — نقلُه بالإسعاف لصنعاء جاء في الخطوة 4 بعد قرار الطبيب في حجة بالخطوة 3.
  • The heart surgeon operated on Anwar in the village clinic. ✗ — العملية الجراحية تمت في الخطوة 5 بعد نقله إلى صنعاء.
  • His parents came to take him home. ✗ — عودة والديه لأخذه هي الخطوة الختامية رقم 7.
