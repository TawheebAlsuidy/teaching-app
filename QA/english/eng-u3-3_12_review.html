3.11 3.12 A business letter
Write a business letter in answer to this job advertisement in the Daily News of April 20th.
Fantastic Job Opportunity!
Have you ever wanted to work on a newspaper?
We are looking for young men and women to train as reporters and photographers for a new general interest magazine for people under 25.
No experience is necessary but the successful applicants must be willing to work hard and already have a wide range of interests.
Write and tell us all about yourself and why you think we should give you the chance to join our exciting team.
Write to: The Editor, The Daily News, PO Box 0055, London S18 9HP

من الكتابu3-3_12-guide · rule · PB ص 24
When writing a letter of application, you must sell yourself, that is, you must give as much interesting information about yourself as you can.
Answer these questions:
• Which school subjects do you like most? Why?
• What interests or hobbies do you have? Give details. If you like music, say which music and why; if you play sport, say which sport and why.
• Have you ever had any work experience? What was it?
• Have you done anything connected with the job itself? What was it?
• Have you travelled? Where to, when and why?
• Do you have any ambitions? What are they?
Remember!
• Lay out your letter correctly.
• Refer to the advertisement.
• Ask for an interview.
• Keep the letter short, but do not miss out anything important.
• Use the correct form of address.

q-3_12-1 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_12-ad
When replying to 'Write to: The Editor, The Daily News, PO Box 0055, London S18 9HP' without knowing the Editor's name, which greeting and closing pair is required by PB page 24?
  • Dear Sir, ... Yours faithfully, ✓
  • Dear Sir, ... Yours sincerely, ✗ — تحية Dear Sir أو Dear Madam لا تجتمع مع Yours sincerely، بل توجب Yours faithfully.
  • Dear Editor Jones, ... Yours faithfully, ✗ — الإعلان لم يذكر اسم المحرر (Jones)، ومتى ذُكر اسم شخص استُعملت Yours sincerely.
  • Dear Mr Editor, ... Best wishes, ✗ — عبارة Best wishes غير رسمية ولا تطابق قواعد الرسالة التجارية في PB ص 24.
q-3_12-2 · mcq · دليل: u3-3_12-guide
According to the 'When writing a letter of application' checklist on PB page 24, what does 'sell yourself' mean?
  • Give as much interesting, specific information about your subjects, hobbies, experience and ambitions as you can while keeping the letter concise. ✓
  • Ask immediately about the starting salary and demand a high wage. ✗ — الإعلان مخصص للتدريب ولا يركّز على المطالبة بالراتب؛ بل ينص الكتاب على شرح sell yourself بتقديم معلومات مشوّقة عنك.
  • Write a very long three-page essay including unrelated personal stories. ✗ — ينص قسم Remember على: Keep the letter short, but do not miss out anything important.
  • Leave out hobbies because employers only read school marks. ✗ — الإعلان يطلب أصحاب اهتمامات واسعة (a wide range of interests)، والصندوق يوصي بتفصيل الهوايات.
