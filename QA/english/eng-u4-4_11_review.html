4.11 4.12 Frozen peas
Read this description of the way peas are frozen. Then do activities A and B in the Workbook.
[Original printed textbook reading card rendered offline below via assets/media/pb32_frozen_peas.webp & full page reference assets/ref/pb_p032.webp; structured anchors for WB 4.11 A–C below:]
Paragraph 1 key facts: Frozen vs market peas — processed within 2–3 hours of being picked/harvested (contrast: ordinary peas sent to market → shops take many hours/days, getting less and less fresh; WB opposites: buy / sent to / many / less and less).
Paragraph 2 processing stages (about half an hour total, quickest = top quality): on arrival at processing factory → softer peas chosen for freezing (harder kept for canning) → cleaned by fans → thoroughly washed → boiling water (1 min) or steam (2–3 min) called 'blanching' → cooled below 20°C → passed through salt water or brine → washed again in clean water → quick-frozen.
Paragraph 3 storage & distribution: packed in big bags → cold storage at -18°C for weeks/months → re-packed in smaller bags → sent in refrigerated trucks to shops and supermarkets.
Use this information to fill in the flow chart in Workbook activity C.

q-4_11-1 · mcq · دليل: u4-4_11-peas-passage
In PB page 32 ('Frozen peas'), what is 'blanching' and how long does it take?
  • Passing peas through boiling water for 1 minute or steam for 2–3 minutes. ✓
  • Storing packed peas in cold storage at -18°C for weeks. ✗ — هذه مرحلة التخزين البارد النهائية لا السلق (blanching).
  • Cleaning dry peas with fans upon factory arrival. ✗ — هذه خطوة التنظيف الأولي بالمراوح قبل الغسل والـ blanching.
  • Packing harder peas into metal cans. ✗ — البازلاء الصلبة تُخصّص للتعليب قبل مرحلة غسل وتصنيع البازلاء المجمدة.

Which factory processing step comes immediately AFTER blanching and cooling below 20°C (WB 4.11 C)?
  • pass through brine (salt water) ✓
