2.10 Language review 4
1 Use of the Present perfect tense
Use the Present perfect tense to describe the present situation.
Example: The worst hurricane in living memory has caused terrible damage.
2 Use of the Past simple tense
Use the Past simple tense to describe something that happened at a known time. Use adverbs to say when the event happened.
Example: The storm hit the area late on Tuesday evening.
من الكتابu2-2_10-relative-adverbs · rule · PB ص 15
3 Prepositions in relative clauses
• Relative clauses giving necessary information
Example: The house is very old. I used to live in it. → The house I used to live in is very old.
Note: The preposition is at the end of the relative clause.
• Relative clauses giving additional information.
Example: Sana’a Secondary School is one of the biggest schools in the country. I went to it when I was younger. → Sana’a Secondary School, to which I went when I was younger, is one of the biggest schools in the country.
Note: The preposition is at the beginning of the relative clause. The clause is written between commas.
4 Adverbs and adverbial phrases
These tell us more about actions.
Examples: (How) People stood around silently. | (Where) In the countryside whole villages have disappeared. | (When) The storm hit the area late on Tuesday evening.

q-2_10-1 · mcq · دليل: u2-2_10-relative-adverbs
According to PB page 15 rule 3, which is the correct way to join: 'The Great Fire of London happened in 1666. Thousands of buildings were destroyed in it.'?
  • The Great Fire of London, in which thousands of buildings were destroyed, happened in 1666. ✓
  • The Great Fire of London which thousands of buildings were destroyed in it happened in 1666. ✗ — يبقى الضمير العائد it زائداً لا يجوز بقاءه في جملة الوصل، كما أن الجملة إضافية تحتاج فاصلتين.
  • The Great Fire of London I thousands of buildings were destroyed in happened in 1666. ✗ — مع المعلومات الإضافية يجب ذكر أداة الوصل (which) مسبوقة بحرف الجر بين فاصلتين.
  • The Great Fire of London happened in 1666 in which. ✗ — موضع عبارة الوصل الإضافية هو بعد الاسم المعرّف مباشرةً بين فاصلتين.
q-2_10-2 · mcq · دليل: u2-2_10-tenses
Why do we write 'took off' (Past simple) in 'The plane ______ from London two days ago' instead of 'has taken off'?
  • Because 'two days ago' names a known finished time in the past. ✓
  • Because 'two days ago' describes the present situation with no known time. ✗ — العكس صحيح: العبارة two days ago تحدد وقتاً ماضياً معلوماً منتهياً، وهذا مجال الماضي البسيط وفق قاعدة 2 في PB ص 15.
  • Because 'take off' never exists in Past simple. ✗ — took off هو الماضي البسيط القياسي غير المنتظم من take off.
  • Because Present perfect is never used in news reports. ✗ — المضارع التام يُستعمل في بداية الأخبار لوصف الوضع الراهن قبل تحديد الزمن كما في الفقرة 1 من 2.7.
